Espionage allegations reach the heart of the chip race, an outside evaluator finds AI is speeding up AI research, and Anthropic ships a new top model.

## What mattered

**Nvidia's CEO is at the centre of a US-China spy scandal.** Jensen Huang admits an affair with a woman US intelligence reportedly believes worked for Beijing. You couldn't make this up. The Information reports, though Reuters couldn't verify it, that the relationship helped move at least $7.5 billion of restricted chips into China. Nvidia lost about $190 billion in a day, its board is investigating, and the Trump administration has questions to answer.

No one has been charged. But if the reports hold up, they show how badly these chips are wanted, and how long a game China is willing to play. [Read more](https://wayintoai.com/ref/nvidia-jensen-huang-china-spy-chips)

![METR chart: Anthropic's benchmark index by model, 2024 to October 2026; the trend steepens from about 15 to about 29 points a year after October 2025, with unreleased internal models at the top](https://wayintoai.com/assets/metr-ai-rd-aeci-main.png)

**METR finds AI is roughly doubling the speed of AI progress at Anthropic.** The independent evaluator estimates Anthropic's models have improved at about twice their earlier rate since October 2025 (plausible range 1.3× to 3×). It guesses about 80% of that is AI doing the research, and saw no bottleneck to stop it compounding over the next year. Anthropic redacted most of the key evidence. [Read more](https://wayintoai.com/ref/metr-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration)

**Anthropic's Fable 5.2 nearly doubled the best score on a remote-work benchmark.** Released on 9 November, ten weeks after Fable 5.1, it makes its biggest gains on long-running agentic tasks. Anthropic still recommends Opus 5.5 for everyday work.
