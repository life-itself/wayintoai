Three big stories this week: espionage allegations around Nvidia's chips, an outside measurement of AI speeding up AI research, and another jump from Anthropic.

## What mattered

**Jensen Huang admits an affair with a woman US intelligence reportedly ties to Beijing.** The New York Times first named her. The Information, citing unnamed sources, says the relationship helped move about $7.5 billion of restricted chips into China; Reuters could not verify that. Export controls are the main US brake on China's AI. This is a big deal. [Read more](https://wayintoai.com/ref/nvidia-jensen-huang-china-spy-chips)

![Chart from METR: Anthropic's internal capability index by model over time; after October 2025 the trend roughly doubles, from about 15 to about 29 points a year](https://wayintoai.com/assets/metr-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration-aeci.png)

**An outside check finds Anthropic's AI progress has roughly doubled in speed.** METR, the independent evaluator, had access to Anthropic's internal data. It estimates Anthropic's models have improved at about 1.9 times their earlier rate since October 2025, and guesses most of that comes from AI doing the research. Anthropic redacted much of the evidence METR found most informative. [Read more](https://wayintoai.com/ref/metr-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration)

**Anthropic's Fable 5.2 nearly doubled the best score on a remote-work benchmark.** Released on 9 November, ten weeks after Fable 5.1, it makes its biggest gains on long-running agentic tasks. Anthropic still recommends Opus 5.5 for everyday work.

| Remote Labor Index | Score |
|---|---|
| **Fable 5.1** | 17.9% |
| **GPT-6 Astra** | 20.8% |
| **Opus 5.5** | 21.3% |
| **Fable 5.2** | 41.3% |

*Anthropic's figures, 9 November 2026.*
