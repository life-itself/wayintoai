A week that read like a thriller: espionage allegations at the heart of the chip race, an independent finding that AI is speeding up AI research, and another leap from Anthropic.

## What mattered

**Nvidia's CEO admits an affair with a woman US intelligence reportedly believes worked for Beijing.** You couldn't make it up. The Information says her network helped move about $7.5 billion of restricted chips into China; Reuters couldn't verify that. Nvidia lost about $190 billion in a day, its board is investigating, and the White House faces questions. It shows how badly China wants these chips, and how long a game it plays. [Read more](https://wayintoai.com/ref/nvidia-jensen-huang-china-spy-chips)

![METR chart: Anthropic's benchmark index by model, 2024 to October 2026; the trend steepens from about 15 to about 29 points a year after October 2025, with unreleased internal models at the top](https://wayintoai.com/assets/metr-ai-rd-aeci-main.png)

**METR finds AI is roughly doubling the speed of AI progress at Anthropic.** After 11 weeks inside the company, the independent evaluator estimates Anthropic's models have improved at about 1.9 times their earlier rate since October 2025. It guesses about 80% of that is AI doing the research, and sees no bottleneck to stop it compounding. Anthropic redacted most of the key evidence. [Read more](https://wayintoai.com/ref/metr-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration)

**Anthropic's Fable 5.2 nearly doubled the best score on a remote-work benchmark.** Released on 9 November, ten weeks after Fable 5.1, it makes its biggest gains on long-running agentic tasks. Anthropic still recommends Opus 5.5 for everyday work.

| Remote Labor Index | Score |
|---|---|
| **Fable 5.1** | 17.9% |
| **GPT-6 Astra** | 20.8% |
| **Opus 5.5** | 21.3% |
| **Fable 5.2** | 41.3% |

*Anthropic's figures, 9 November 2026.*
