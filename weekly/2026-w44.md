This week brought more evidence for what I think of as AI's security apocalypse: bugs found faster than anyone can fix them. Also, Anthropic filed to go public.

## What mattered

**A flaw in PHP, the language under most of the web, sent governments offline.** PHP runs about 69% of websites whose server language is known, WordPress included. This was the worst kind of bug: anyone could take over a server without logging in.

Japan's prime minister told organisations to patch that night or consider taking their sites offline. Dutch and Finnish government sites went dark, and the FBI and NSA issued warnings.

PHP was one of four critical failures in four days, alongside GitLab, the Railway cloud host and a certificate authority. AI is speeding up both finding and exploiting bugs, and the PHP Foundation says AI-assisted reports are flooding it. Anthropic's deputy CISO, Jason Clinton, says things will get "a lot worse before they get better", for about 18 months.

[Read more](https://wayintoai.com/ref/black-october-2026). On defence, [Claude proved over 100 libraries crash-free, in a modelled setup](https://wayintoai.com/ref/anthropic-formal-verification). OpenAI [set its own agents to attack its training sandboxes](https://wayintoai.com/ref/openai-containment-red-teaming), and they escaped; two passed messages by timing the CPU cache. All of it: [the AI cyber crunch](https://wayintoai.com/ref/moc-ai-security-incidents).

**Anthropic has filed to go public; backers reportedly want $2 trillion or more.** About double May's $965bn private round. The filing shows $16.3bn of revenue in the first half of 2026, up 1,300% on a year earlier. [Read more](https://wayintoai.com/ref/anthropic-ipo-s1)

![LinkedIn post by physicist Christopher Herzog describing a paper written by AI agents](https://wayintoai.com/assets/herzog-stergiou-ai-cft-linkedin.jpg)

**AI agents picked a physics question, worked it through and wrote the paper.** Two King's College London physicists asked ChatGPT and Claude to pick a question worth a Physical Review Letters paper. The agents chose the idea and drafted it in a couple of hours; checking took two weeks. [Read more](https://wayintoai.com/ref/herzog-stergiou-ai-physics-cft)

## One thing to try

**Start each AI session fresh, and file as you go.** A session is a desk, not a filing cabinet: long chats get slow and muddled. Keep what matters in files, and let each fresh session read them.

First step: at the end of today's chat, say "Write down what we worked out and where to start next time, as a short note I can save." Tomorrow, give that note to a new chat. Two minutes. [Read the note](https://wayintoai.com/use/knowledge-work/memory-on-disk)

## The bigger picture

![Goldman Sachs charts: US industries with more AI adoption show lower employment growth and higher unemployment](https://wayintoai.com/assets/goldman-ai-exposure-employment-2026-10.png)

**AI's effect on jobs is starting to show beyond tech.** A Goldman Sachs note finds US industries using more AI have slower job growth and higher unemployment, a correlation, not proof of cause. [Read more](https://wayintoai.com/ref/goldman-ai-adoption-employment)

At Anthropic, which calls itself a leading indicator, agents now work six hours for every human hour, and juniors fell from 27% to 9% of technical hires. [Read more](https://wayintoai.com/ref/anthropic-employment-at-anthropic)
