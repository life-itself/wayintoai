This week agent work started to go multiplayer, and Opus 5.5 kept making videos.

## What mattered

**Two people can now steer one AI agent session.** Zed's Delta, in public beta, lets several people join one agent thread to watch, steer and review while it works. A week earlier [we had found nothing that did this](https://wayintoai.com/posts/2026-09-27-live-collaborate-on-one-ai-agent-session). [Read more](https://wayintoai.com/ref/delta-zed)

**Claude can now coordinate its own parallel threads.** In the redesigned Claude Projects, one conversation hands tasks to separate Claude Code cloud sessions and assembles the results. It works only with GitHub, and you can't share a project with a colleague. [Read more](https://wayintoai.com/ref/claude-projects)

![Title card of the Opus 5.5 video "AI is a normal technology?"](https://wayintoai.com/assets/opus-5-5-normal-technology-video.jpg)

**Opus 5.5 makes watchable videos, given the right workflow.** "AI is a normal technology?" is a five-minute rap video made by Opus 5.5 with music from Suno. The trick, [per Rexan Wong](https://wayintoai.com/ref/rexan-wong-motion-graphics-prompt): name a reference video, render with code, and approve storyboards before anything animates. [Watch it](https://wayintoai.com/ref/opus-5-5-ai-normal-technology-music-video)

## One thing to try

![ASD-STE100 at a glance: document structure, sentence rules, verb forms, dictionary entries and word limits](https://wayintoai.com/assets/asd-ste100-overview.png)

**A controlled-English spec makes AI explanations easier to read.** Andrej Karpathy asks his LLM to explain things in ASD-STE100, a specification first written for aerospace maintenance manuals, and finds the result often a lot more readable. On the next answer you can't follow, reply: "Explain that again in ASD-STE100, about 80% of the way." [Read more](https://wayintoai.com/ref/karpathy-efficient-ways-to-get-llms-to-explain)

## The bigger picture

Some Opus 5.5 videos argue about AI itself. The "normal technology" rap asks sceptics: "what's your forecast when the hammer starts to build the hand?" [Bright Mirror's video](https://wayintoai.com/ref/bright-mirror-nothing-went-foom) tells doomers that "NOTHING WENT FOOM".
