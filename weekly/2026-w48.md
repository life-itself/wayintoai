Two things this week: Claude helped settle perhaps the most famous open problem in mathematics, and nearly 2,000 people signed up to march on Downing Street for a pause.

## What mattered

![Rufus Pollock in his YouTube short, captioned "The end of human mathematics. Everything else is next."](https://wayintoai.com/assets/riemann-short-thumb.jpg)

**Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge and Claude have proved the Riemann Hypothesis.** It took about six weeks and was released only as Lean code, with no human-readable paper. On 21 November the Lean FRO and the Mathlib Initiative certified the statement and proof as correct. I think this is massive: the end of human mathematics, and everything else is next. [Read more](https://wayintoai.com/ref/riemann-hypothesis-proved) · [Watch my short](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/rOWKmYk4_eQ) · [AI and maths timeline](https://wayintoai.com/ref/ai-and-mathematics)

**PauseAI UK marched on Downing Street on 21 November, billed as the largest AI safety march yet.** Nearly 2,000 registered; organisers say over 1,000 came, while Anadolu reported "hundreds". February's march drew a little over 300. Placards read "Just don't build AGI". Still small, but growing, and carrying the same message as [the insiders](https://wayintoai.com/ref/moc-ai-risk-warnings): slow down. [Read more](https://wayintoai.com/ref/pauseai-london-march-nov-2026)

**Historian Mark Humphries says what happened to maths is reaching history.** GPT-6.2 Astra took 22 minutes to name the unknown artist of an 1808 sketchbook and pointed him to a forgotten 162-page diary that proved it. He puts historians where mathematicians were eleven months ago, when LLMs began solving Erdős problems. [Read more](https://wayintoai.com/ref/humphries-gpt-6-2-historical-discovery)

## The bigger picture

**Anthropic's economists find AI's biggest gains at work come with reorganising, and that the same change drives worker resistance.** From 66 interviews at 58 US customer firms (not a representative sample): very few AI layoffs, but most firms are hiring less, and many cut contractors. [Read more](https://wayintoai.com/ref/anthropic-working-with-ai)
