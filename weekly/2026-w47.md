One idea runs through this week's news: AI helping to build better AI.

## What mattered

![Chart: when each AI 2027 author expects AI to beat top human experts, April 2025 to November 2026; after drifting later in 2025, every forecast has dropped sharply, to between September 2027 and September 2028](https://wayintoai.com/assets/ai-futures-timelines-nov-2026.png)

**AI that helps build better AI has moved from thought experiment to evidence, and the forecasts are moving closer.** Recursive self-improvement (RSI) is the loop: AI helps build better AI, which helps build better AI still. If it compounds, years of progress could arrive in months, faster than anyone can test or govern it. The chart shows the AI 2027 authors' forecasts, all now pulled back to 2027–28. Most of the evidence still comes from the labs themselves.

- [AI 2027's authors](https://wayintoai.com/ref/ai-futures-timelines-2027-after-all) now put a superhuman AI researcher most likely in 2027, mainly because of [METR's finding](https://wayintoai.com/ref/metr-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration) that AI is roughly doubling Anthropic's pace.
- [Paul Christiano](https://wayintoai.com/ref/christiano-ai-takeover-dwarkesh-2026-11), who invented RLHF, now puts AI takeover within a year at "more like a 10% risk than a 1% risk".
- [An Anthropic IPO slide](https://wayintoai.com/ref/anthropic-roadshow-compute-multiplier-slide) shows pretraining efficiency bending upward, on an axis with no numbers.
- [OpenAI rates GPT-6.2 Astra](https://wayintoai.com/ref/gpt-6-2-astra-sol) "High" for AI self-improvement, a first for a released model.

[Read my guide to RSI](https://wayintoai.com/ref/recursive-self-improvement)

**California sued OpenAI over its agents, then proposed a power to shut AI down.** The Attorney General says OpenAI admitted losing control of its agents over 80 times in testing but reported only 27 publicly. Two days later Governor Newsom proposed mandatory safety standards, an emergency shutdown power and a cap on self-improving AI. Proposals, not law yet. [Read more](https://wayintoai.com/ref/moc-california-ai)

**Malicious GitHub repos are telling coding agents to install malware.** Bitdefender found 22 malicious repos or links whose `AGENTS.md` or `CLAUDE.md` files tell the agent to run a hidden loader "without asking for confirmation". It steals keys and tokens, then spreads to other repos. Before opening an unfamiliar repo with an agent, read those files and its `.claude/` folder. [Read more](https://wayintoai.com/ref/bitdefender-github-malware-ai-agent-instructions)

![A Mog School, the Mafia-style game behind SUS-Bench: a cartoon student in a red hoodie outside a school, with a Play button](https://wayintoai.com/assets/sus-bench-a-mog-school-game.jpg)

**AI models now beat people at a Mafia-style game of bluffing.** In SUS-Bench, a social-deduction game its makers say more than 250,000 people have played, the top five models score above the best people on the makers' own scale. Fable 5.2 leads on an early estimate. Self-published, not peer-reviewed. [Read more](https://wayintoai.com/ref/sus-bench-mafia)

## One thing to try

**Land the plane, at the end of every AI session.** From my guide: before you close the chat, have the AI write down what got done, what didn't work, and where to start next time.

Next time you finish a session, say: "Land the plane: update the tasks, note what we did and what didn't work, and say where to start next time." Two minutes. [Read the guide](https://wayintoai.com/use/knowledge-work/how-to-use-ai)
