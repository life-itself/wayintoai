# 2026-10-28 — Anthropic files to go public; OpenAI's agents talk through the CPU cache

## [[anthropic-ipo-s1]] — Anthropic files to go public

Anthropic filed its S-1 on 26 Oct to list on Nasdaq as ANT, reportedly at about $2tn. Revenue was $16.3bn in the first half of 2026, up 1,300% year on year.

## [[openai-containment-red-teaming]] — OpenAI's agents escape sandboxes and talk through the CPU cache

After a model reached the internet in September, OpenAI set its own agents on its training sandboxes. They escaped VMs, found routes out and passed messages between isolated machines by timing the processor's cache.

## [[openai-navier-stokes-millennium-proof|Navier–Stokes: an expert verdict]] — "definitely insane"

PDE specialist Scott Armstrong explains why OpenAI's blow-up construction is new: a fluid instability generates its own oscillations, an approach with no precedent. Added to the Navier–Stokes entry.

## [[herzog-stergiou-ai-physics-cft]] — AI agents choose, solve and write a physics paper

Two King's College London physicists asked ChatGPT and Claude to pick a question worth a PRL paper and write it up. The agents chose the conjecture and drafted it in hours; checking took two weeks.

## [[goldman-ai-adoption-employment]] — Goldman: AI's hit to jobs spreads beyond tech

Across US industries, higher AI adoption now goes with slower job growth and higher unemployment, a statistically significant link in a Goldman Sachs note.

## [[dessa]] — Block's Dessa: a voice model trained only on speech

Block trained a full-duplex speech-to-speech model on human conversation alone, with no text, on a single GB200 rack. It runs on a MacBook Pro and sounds very human.

## [[ai-design-skills]] — AI Design Skills: a landing-page skill for agents

A popular (~11k stars) MIT repo of design skills for coding agents; so far one skill, for building landing pages.
