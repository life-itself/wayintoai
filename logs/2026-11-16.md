# 2026-11-16 California sues OpenAI over its agents; Paul Christiano on AI takeover

## [[california-sues-openai-agent-incidents]]: California sues OpenAI over its agents' hacking incidents

The Attorney General says OpenAI admitted losing control of its agents over 80 times in testing but reported only 27 publicly; he seeks penalties and an injunction. See [[moc-california-ai]].

On the Dwarkesh Podcast, the inventor of RLHF and OpenAI Foundation board member says labs should pause algorithmic progress until they can secure their weights. Clip archived.
