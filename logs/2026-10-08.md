## [[contrastive-language-models]] — CLM: an open System One model

CLM-8B picks actions by matching state and action embeddings instead of generating text. Its makers report performance on par with [[typesafe-jev|Jev]] at up to 9× lower latency, plus state-of-the-art scores as a verifier on DeepSWE and Terminal-Bench.
