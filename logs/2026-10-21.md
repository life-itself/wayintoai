# 2026-10-21 — Anthropic launches Claude Duet

## [[claude-duet]] — Claude Duet: Anthropic's proactive personal agent

A separate app (US waitlist, Pro and Max) that briefs you each morning, works in its own cloud computer while you're away, and asks before it sends, pays or signs anything. It is the fourth big-lab [[moc-personal-agents|personal agent]] in ten weeks, after Grok Bot, Muse and Dots.
