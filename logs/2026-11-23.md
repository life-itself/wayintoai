# 2026-11-23 Anthropic: AI value comes from reorganising work; Jensen Huang back at Nvidia

## [[anthropic-working-with-ai]]: Working with AI

Anthropic interviewed 66 people at 58 US companies: the highest-value AI uses involve real organisational change, which also drives worker resistance. Few AI layoffs, but less hiring and fewer contractors.

## [[nvidia-jensen-huang-reinstated]]: Jensen Huang reinstated as Nvidia CEO

Eleven days after resigning, Huang is back after the board got assurances from the US government; CNBC says Trump sealed the deal. The China investigations continue.
