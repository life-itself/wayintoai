# 2026-10-26 — AI is already superhuman in physics; Anthropic files for IPO

## [[herzog-stergiou-ai-physics-cft]] — AI agents choose, solve and write a physics paper

Two King's College London physicists asked ChatGPT and Claude to pick a question worth a PRL paper and write it up. The agents chose the conjecture and drafted it in hours; checking took two weeks.

Backers reportedly target at least $2tn, double May's $965bn private round. The S-1 shows $16.3bn revenue in the first half of 2026, up 1,300% year on year.

## [[goldman-ai-adoption-employment]] — Goldman: AI's hit to jobs spreads beyond tech

Across US industries, higher AI adoption now goes with slower job growth and higher unemployment, a statistically significant link in a Goldman Sachs note.
