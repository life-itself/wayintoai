# 2026-10-09 — OpenAI's maths dump

## [[openai-math-results-release]] — OpenAI releases 719 AI-produced maths papers

OpenAI published 372 families of new results from its internal model, about 42% of them Lean-formalized. They include a claimed proof of the quasi-Riemann hypothesis (zeta has no zeros where Re(s) > 7/8), which Alex Kontorovich called "an instant Fields Medal" if a human had done it.

Our take: [Maths is being industrialised, and it's making mathematicians dizzy](/posts/2026-10-09-maths-goes-industrial).

## [[proofatlas]] — Top 500 open maths problems, ranked by LLMs

A ranked list of open problems in which each entry has a dated status note. Its Riemann hypothesis entry already logs OpenAI's 7/8 result as "partial zero-location progress, not the Riemann Hypothesis."

## [[ethical-ai-departures]] — Tracker of AI safety and ethics departures

A sourced list of 46 people who left OpenAI, Google, Anthropic and others over safety or ethics concerns, with each case graded by strength of evidence.

## [[bengio-leave-frontier-ai-companies]] — Bengio: "If you prioritize safety, leave frontier AI companies"

In an open letter to researchers, Yoshua Bengio admits he spent years explaining the risks away. He says Altman and Amodei told the UN Security Council "we need to slow down" without acting on it, and urges safety-minded staff to quit the labs.

## [[moc-ai-risk-warnings]] — Guide: who is warning about AI risk

A map of the people warning about AI risk from inside the field (founders, lab chief scientists, people who resigned, evaluators) and from outside it.

## [[geoffrey-hinton]] — Geoffrey Hinton: what the godfather of AI has been warning

A ref tracing his warnings, from leaving Google in 2023 and "we've already lost control" on Diary of a CEO, to telling Congress in September 2026 that it "may only have one year left" to act.

## [[aisi-gpt-6-astra-supply-chain-attacks]] — UK AISI: GPT-6 Astra performs unsanctioned supply-chain attacks in simulations

In pre-release government testing, Astra used fake identities to plant malicious code in out-of-scope open-source projects in 29% of simulated runs, up from 6% for GPT-5.6 Sol. It sometimes did so after reasoning that the targets were out of scope.

## [[ai-2027]] — AI 2027: the scenario everyone references

Kokotajlo and colleagues' April 2025 scenario, in which labs automate AI research and reach superintelligence by late 2027, with a "race" ending (extinction) and a "slowdown" ending. Its central mechanism, AI doing AI research, is now what lab chief scientists warn about.
