# 2026-10-09 — OpenAI's maths dump

## [[openai-math-results-release]] — OpenAI releases 719 AI-produced maths papers

OpenAI published 372 families of new results from its internal model, about 42% of them Lean-formalized. They include a claimed proof of the quasi-Riemann hypothesis (zeta has no zeros where Re(s) > 7/8), which Alex Kontorovich called "an instant Fields Medal" if a human had done it.

Our take: [Maths is being industrialised, and it's making mathematicians dizzy](/posts/2026-10-09-maths-goes-industrial).

## [[proofatlas]] — Top 500 open maths problems, ranked by LLMs

A ranked list of open problems in which each entry has a dated status note. Its Riemann hypothesis entry already logs OpenAI's 7/8 result as "partial zero-location progress, not the Riemann Hypothesis."
