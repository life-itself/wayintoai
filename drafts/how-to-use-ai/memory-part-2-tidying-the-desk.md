# AI memory part 2: tidying the desk

Writing things down does three different jobs:

1. **Filing (long-term memory).** The main point, already in part 1. After working something out across papers on the desk, file the distilled result in the cabinet: a briefing for the next project, how the design works, key decisions, plans, tasks, a log corrected over time. Conclusions, not working papers. This is what lets many desks share one project: several sessions at once, or over weeks.
2. **A note left on the desk (short-term holdover).** The cleaners clear the desk overnight, so leave a hand-off note for tomorrow, another stream of work or a colleague. Still useful, but less needed now that compaction is good.
3. **A working sheet (within one session).** The AI writes back and forth to a document rather than relying on the whole conversation history. Cleaner even mid-session.

**Compaction is tidying the desk:** binning papers and rewriting notes more compactly. It has got much better and cuts the need for hand-off notes. But it doesn't share anything across desks, and a long session still gets messy.
