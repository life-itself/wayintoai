# Anthropic files for IPO at a ~$2 trillion valuation

On 26 October 2026 Anthropic filed its S-1 to list on Nasdaq as ANT. Backers are reported to be targeting a valuation of at least $2 trillion, roughly double the $965 billion of its last private round in May. The filing shows revenue growing more than tenfold a year.

## The numbers

| Period | Revenue | Net loss | of which: fair-value changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2023 | $41m | $1.5bn | $1.0bn |
| 2024 | $386m | $8.3bn | $5.4bn |
| 2025 | $4.6bn | $42.0bn | $34.3bn |
| H1 2026 | $16.3bn (+1,300% year on year) | $80.7bn | $72.4bn |

*Source: Anthropic S-1, risk factors.*

Most of the headline loss is an accounting effect: as Anthropic's valuation rose, so did the value of its convertible notes and SAFEs, and that increase counts as a loss. Strip it out and the loss in the first half of 2026 is about $8bn on $16bn of revenue.

Customers paying $100,000 or more a year rose from over 950 to over 11,900 between September 2025 and September 2026. Around half of revenue (48% in H1 2026) comes through cloud marketplaces such as AWS and Google Cloud.

## Governance

Anthropic stays a public benefit corporation. Its Long-Term Benefit Trust, a body of financially disinterested trustees, holds a special Class T stock that lets it elect and remove some directors. Employees get a separate Class E stock with limited votes and no economic rights. Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley lead the offering; price and share count are not yet set.

## Why it matters

A safety-focused lab is about to answer to public shareholders. The filing is also the first audited look at how fast frontier AI revenue is really growing.
