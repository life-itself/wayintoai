# Victory By Any Means

A short video about AI existential risk and the arms-race dynamics that push competitors to pursue increasingly dangerous capabilities in order to avoid losing to one another.

![Victory By Any Means](https://screenshotit.app/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RBURmy9YpRo)

## Overview

The central danger is not necessarily that every participant ignores the risks, but that each believes restraint will hand victory to a less cautious rival. “Victory by any means” becomes a coordination failure: competitive pressure makes individually understandable choices add up to catastrophic collective risk.
