# Jensen Huang reinstated as Nvidia CEO

**Eleven days after he resigned, Nvidia's board unanimously reinstated Jensen Huang as president, CEO and board member on 23 November 2026, after input from "senior levels of the United States government".**

## What happened

- **Confirmed (Nvidia)**: the board says it was "satisfied with respect to Mr. Huang's candor and ability to lead", and that on government input it is confident his return "poses no national security risk". Harvey C. Jones stays as chair; Colette Kress goes back to CFO. The independent investigation into reports that Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort continues, and a new National Security and Export Control Committee of independent directors oversees trade compliance.
- **Reported**: CNBC says a personal assurance from President Trump that Huang had not broken national security laws sealed the decision. This reverses the board's position of a week earlier that it would not reinstate him.
- **Reported**: Nvidia lost about $1.2 trillion in market value while he was out; shares rallied on the news. California's Department of Justice is probing whether chips or confidential information went to China, and Huang is expected before Congress in December.

## Why it matters

The quick return shows how far Nvidia, and the AI chip supply it controls, now runs through Washington: the White House helped decide who runs the company. The China and export-control questions behind his exit are still open.
