# Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO

On 12 November 2026 Nvidia announced that Jensen Huang had resigned as president, CEO and board member, "effective immediately", after 33 years running the company he co-founded. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO while the board searches for a permanent one. Harvey C. Jones, former Synopsys CEO, becomes Nvidia's first-ever board chair.

This follows [[nvidia-jensen-huang-china-spy-chips]]: Huang's admitted affair with Alice Huang, a woman US intelligence reportedly believes worked for Beijing, and reports that the relationship helped move restricted chips into China.

## What is confirmed and what is reported

- **Confirmed (Nvidia)**: the resignation, Kress as interim CEO, Jones as chair. The release gives no reason. Press reports say Nvidia's SEC filing describes no disagreement with the board.
- **Reported**: that the exit followed the affair Huang admitted in an 8 November staff memo, and reports of another relationship with an executive who reported to him directly. The board's investigation, run by the law firm Wachtell Lipton, is still going; Nvidia says it has no reason to believe national security or company secrets were compromised.
- **Reported (16–17 Nov)**: the board does not intend to reinstate him, though Huang says he would be "honored" to return and President Trump said he was open to it.
- **Markets**: shares fell sharply on the news and for several days after. In its first results since, on 17 November, Nvidia beat profit and revenue forecasts.
- **Not reported**: any criminal charge against Huang, or evidence that US secrets reached Beijing.

## Why it matters

Nvidia makes the chips the AI race runs on, and Huang has led it since 1993. A sudden exit at the top of the most valuable chip company, tied to a counter-intelligence story, puts export controls and chip security at the centre of AI politics.
