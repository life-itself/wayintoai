# March Against AI Extinction: PauseAI's London march

**On 21 November 2026 PauseAI UK marched from All Souls' Place to Downing Street, billed as the largest AI safety march yet. Organisers say over 1,000 people came.**

![PauseAI UK: March Against AI Extinction](https://screenshotit.app/https://pauseai.uk/protests/march-against-ai-extinction-nov-26)

## Overview

The march called for a halt to frontier AI development. Placards included "Just don't build AGI" and "AI is an extinction threat". Nearly 2,000 people had registered beforehand. Turnout is disputed: organisers and supporters say over 1,000, while Anadolu reported "hundreds", and there was no independent count. PauseAI's February London march drew a little over 300.

## Why it matters

Street protest against AI is still small, but it is growing, and it now carries the same message as the insiders' warnings: slow down.
