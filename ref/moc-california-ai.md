# California and AI: a map

**TL;DR:** California is where the US is regulating frontier AI. Most of the big labs are based there, Congress hasn't legislated, and the state has moved from a vetoed safety bill (2024) to disclosure rules (2025) to suing a lab and proposing mandatory standards, a shutdown power and limits on self-improving AI (November 2026). The open question is whether Washington pre-empts it.

## Why California matters

Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind's US arm, Meta and xAI are all headquartered or largely based in California, so its laws reach the frontier labs directly. With no federal AI law, the state's rules have become the de facto US baseline, which is why the White House is trying to override them.

## Timeline

| Date | Action |
|---|---|
| Sep 2024 | **SB 1047 vetoed.** Newsom vetoes the first frontier-AI safety bill and asks for an expert-led approach instead. |
| Jun 2025 | **Expert report.** The Joint California Policy Working Group on AI Frontier Models publishes its report, the basis for SB 53. |
| Sep 2025 | **SB 53 signed** (Transparency in Frontier AI Act, in force 1 Jan 2026): frontier developers must publish safety frameworks, report critical safety incidents and protect whistleblowers. |
| Dec 2025 | **Federal push-back.** A Trump executive order directs the Justice Department to challenge state AI laws.* |
| Jul–Sep 2026 | **The Hugging Face incident**, then an Attorney General investigation of OpenAI ([[openai-agent-swarm-hugging-face-breach]]). |
| Sep 2026 | **Auditors and a kill switch.** SB 813 and AB 1405 create certified independent verifiers and a registry of AI auditors.* Executive Order N-9-26 speeds them up, calls for an AI "kill switch" and adds loss-of-control incidents to the critical incidents labs must report. |
| 16 Nov 2026 | **The state sues OpenAI** over its agents' breaches, citing over 80 losses of control in testing: [[california-sues-openai-agent-incidents]]. |
| 18 Nov 2026 | **Mandatory standards proposed**, with emergency shutdown and powers to cap recursive self-improvement: [[california-ai-safety-next-steps-2026-11]]. |

*\* From secondary sources; check against the primary text before relying on the detail.*

Other California AI laws in force from 2026 include rules for companion chatbots (SB 243) and training-data transparency (AB 2013).*

## The fight with Washington

The Trump administration wants a single national rule that pre-empts states; Newsom asks Congress to treat California's framework as "a floor, not a ceiling". As of November 2026 Congress has not passed pre-emption, and we have not seen a federal suit against SB 53.
