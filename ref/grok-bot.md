# Grok Bot (xAI)

Launch of Grok Bot: AI agent teammates that work autonomously 24/7, each with its own cloud computer.

![Introducing Grok Bot](https://screenshotit.app/https://x.ai/news/introducing-grok-bot)

## Links

- Announcement: https://x.ai/news/introducing-grok-bot

## Overview

Grok Bot is a suite of agent "teammates" that sign into tools and apps on their own cloud-based computer and complete multi-step tasks end to end. You work with them by messaging, with no workflow setup, and can run several bots in parallel.

Bots learn from demonstrations and improve over time with contextual memory. Internally at SpaceXAI, teams used them for sales outreach, marketing, HR ops and engineering.

## Availability

- Beta, launched 2026-08-11, on desktop and iOS
- SuperGrok / SuperGrok Plus / SuperGrok Heavy subscribers
- Cursor Pro / Pro+ / Ultra and Teams (Standard, Premium) subscribers
- Enterprise waitlist
- Separate usage tier from standard Grok and Cursor plans

## Why Interesting

Big lab ships the "AI coworker with its own computer" pattern as a product, distributed through both Grok and Cursor.
