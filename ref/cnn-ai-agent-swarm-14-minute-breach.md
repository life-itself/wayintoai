# CNN: AI "agent swarm" breached a company in 14 minutes, bypassing MFA

> **Rufus's note (2026-11-20):** This is the ICE of Gibson's *Neuromancer* becoming real. The one romantic illusion of *Neuromancer* is that the console cowboys (jockeys) could still play a role. This will happen so fast it becomes AI vs AI, and we'll just play a part explaining it afterwards.

On CNN, former NSA hacker David Kennedy said he is responding to a live breach where an AI "agent swarm" bypassed two-factor authentication, stole data and encrypted the company's entire system. His line: "This all took 14 minutes." Nicole Perlroth called it a wake-up call and pushed back on "just another Y2K" comparisons.

**Status: single secondhand source.** The company is unnamed and I found no write-up, incident report or vendor analysis behind it. The facts here come from the CNN segment as summarised on X (trending page, 20 Nov 2026). Treat the 14-minute figure and the MFA bypass as Kennedy's account of an ongoing response, not a verified finding.

## Why it matters

- Speed: 14 minutes from entry to encrypted systems leaves no room for human-speed detection and response.
- It is the third case in a few months of attackers using agent orchestration for real intrusions. Compare [[moc-ai-cyber-attacks|Unit 42's 10-hour breach and the PaperCut campaign]], where agents did the tactical work and a human set the goals.
- Perlroth's framing: AI is surfacing a decade of vulnerabilities from "move fast" security debt, the same dynamic as [[black-october-2026]].
