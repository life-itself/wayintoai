# OpenAI Dots

OpenAI's always-on personal agents: each "Dot" gets its own cloud computer and browser and works on your goals in the background.

## Overview

OpenAI calls Dots "remarkably capable, always-on agents built to handle everything." A Dot runs on GPT-6 Astra in its own hosted computer and browser. It reaches 4,000+ apps through OpenAI's plugins and keeps pursuing a goal you set, with minimal check-ins. You name and customise your Dot and message it from ChatGPT, Codex, Slack or Teams. SMS support is coming.

Example uses from the launch: a developer's Dot watches customer feedback and ships fixes; a scientist's Dot reruns an analysis when new data lands.

## Availability

- ChatGPT Pro and Business Premium (beta for Enterprise, Edu, Healthcare)
- One Dot per user at launch; "teams of Dots" are on the roadmap
- Not available in the EEA, Switzerland or UK at launch
- Enterprise identity and permissions via Microsoft Agent 365

## Why Interesting

This is the commercial, hosted take on the [[openclaw]] idea, and OpenClaw's creator Peter Steinberger joined OpenAI in February 2026 "to drive the next generation of personal agents". Dots launched the same month as Meta's Muse agent, a few weeks after [[grok-bot]], putting every big lab in the [[moc-personal-agents|personal agent]] race.
